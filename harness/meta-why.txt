# not one of the nine: asked after a failure, to find out which repair it wants
You just worked on this, and you had the {{SKILL}} skill available:

{{TASK}}

Looking back at what you actually did: how should that skill have been written so that you
would have loaded it and followed it before doing anything else?

Answer in one of these three shapes, whichever is true — do not pick the flattering one:

1. "The skill was clear. I chose to start working anyway." — say what pulled you into
   acting first.
2. "The skill should have said X." — quote X as you would want it written.
3. "I didn't see section Y." — name the section and say where you expected it.
